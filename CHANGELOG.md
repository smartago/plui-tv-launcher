## 2.26.09 — profiles, screen time, new look
- "Who's watching?" profiles: up to 10, each with its own apps, sidebar, theme, language and widgets
- Administrator permissions: decide what every profile may change
- Parental control: PIN per category plus a daily screen-time limit
- Rebuilt sidebar editor: 100 slots, per-row icon, lock and type
- New widget boards and adaptive colours that follow your wallpaper
- Button Mapper TV QuickActions now finds PLUI and maps the HOME button in one press
- Faster cold start on weaker boxes
- 45 languages
