# FAQ

**Is it really free? What is "Elite"?**
Yes. Elite is the full edition — every widget, every wallpaper source, unlimited sidebar rows, profiles, cameras. It is included at no cost. No account, no subscription, no ads, no sponsored rows.

**Does it replace my TV's home screen?**
Yes, that is the point. Your apps stay exactly where they are; only the screen around them changes. You can go back to the stock launcher any time — see [home-button.md](home-button.md).

**HOME still opens the old launcher.**
[home-button.md](home-button.md) covers every case, including the boxes that hard-wire the key.

**Will it work on my device?**
Android 6.0 or newer, any Android TV / Google TV / Fire TV / TV box. If the Play Store calls your device unsupported, sideload instead — [install.md](install.md).

**Does it work on a phone or tablet?**
It is built for the TV and a remote. It installs on a phone, but that is not what it is for.

**What data does it collect?**
No tracking, no advertising IDs, nothing sold. The app talks to our server for its content (wallpapers, weather, widget lists) and for anonymous activation/crash counts. Cloud backup is opt-in and keyed to a Device ID you can see in Options › Tools.

**Can I back up my setup?**
Options › Tools › Cloud backup. One Device ID restores your sidebar, widgets, profiles and theme on a new box.

**Which languages?**
45, picked on first run and changeable per profile.

**Does it need accessibility permission?**
Only as a fallback for the HOME key on devices with no default-launcher setting. It is never required for the launcher itself.

**Why do my IP cameras show "connected" but the picture is black?**
Two usual causes: the stream is H.265 on a box whose decoder is already busy (restart the box), or the camera only serves a sub-stream on another path. Check the RTSP URL in Options › Cameras.

**Something is broken / I have an idea.**
[Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) with your device model and Android version, or email support@smartago.net.
